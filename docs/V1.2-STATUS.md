# v1.2: status and next steps

Working notes for continuing the v1.2 work on branch `claude/inspiring-einstein-er2d37`.
1.2.0-beta.1, -beta.2 and -beta.3 were published as pre-releases (as MacBridge); 1.2.0 is the
first release as **Taro** (CHANGELOG.md says what each has).

## Done (on the branch)

- Sound end to end (Mac capture, sealed UDP/TCP audio, WASAPI playback, jitter buffer).
- Typed address (IPv4/IPv6/name, `--direct`, Mac `--port`, dual-stack listener).
- Scoped `open_file`, drag and drop to open, adoption of apps opened on the Mac.
- Motion system, Liquid Glass (MobileLab, MIT), loading window, glass menu, Taro Search,
  reconnect banner, connection phases, keyboard modes, Settings entries.
- The title bar and glass menus of each Mac app's window (beta 3).
- The name Taro, its violet and its icon (a taro on violet, `logo.rs`).
- The Dock at a side of the screen, out on a push against the edge (`dock.rs`); the Mac's own
  Dock is no longer streamed (Desktop Fusion and its wallpaper sync are gone).
- Full screen over the taskbar, the taskbar up at the bottom edge; pictures a few pixels off
  their window fill it, bands of another shape are never see-through.
- Quit and reopen at once (the Mac asks processes, not `isTerminated`); e2e `relaunch` checks.
- Captures started again when macOS stops them; the Mac's menu bar and Dock settings put back
  after a crash.
- `taro.sh` launcher + `taro --check-permissions`; release tarball ships it.

## Not verified by CI (real hardware)

Real speakers, real monitors and scales, the raw-input push on real mice and touchpads, the
taskbar coming up over a full-screen window on a real desktop (CI notes it as
`taskbar: up at the bottom edge=…`), multi-monitor edges, the launcher on a fresh Mac.
